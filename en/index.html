How it works

Your plan for every day

  1. 01

    Choose your level and pace

    A short test shows where to start. The pace is yours: 10, 15 or 20 new words a day.

  2. 02

    Open today’s plan

    Review, new words, practice, a text or a dialogue. Step by step — no need to decide what’s next.

  3. 03

    Learn new words

    A picture, an example sentence and a natural voice. Swipe right — I know it; left — not yet.

  4. 04

    Make a mistake, learn why

    A short rule and an example right away. The word comes back exactly when it’s time to review it.

Who makes NightLex

An independent project for people who really want to learn

NightLex is a small independent project: no investors, no ads, no selling of data. We are building a game that makes it easy to learn Danish every day — with clear explanations, real texts and a pace you choose. It started as a game for Ukrainians; the English interface opens it to everyone learning Danish. Write to us right from the game: “Me” → “Report a problem”.

FAQ

What people usually ask

Is it free?

Yes — during testing everything is free and ad-free.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open it and learn — your progress is stored on your device.

I’m a complete beginner. Is it for me?

Yes. Choose “I’m a complete beginner” and start from the very first word. If you already know some, the level test counts the words you know.

Which languages can I learn?

Danish and US English with a Ukrainian interface. Danish can also be learned with an English interface.

Why is the content made with AI?

That’s how we could prepare thousands of words, sentences and texts for every level. Everything was double-checked by AI, and native speakers are now checking it line by line — we fix every mistake.

How much time does it take a day?

Up to 20 minutes on Relaxed, up to 30 on Fast and up to an hour on Intensive. You can change the pace any time.
