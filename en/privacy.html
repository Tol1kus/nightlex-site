Privacy

Updated 22 November 2026

NightLex collects as little as possible — only what learning needs. No ads, analytics, trackers or cookies. We never ask for or store your email, passwords or payment details.

This website (nightlex.com)

The page only remembers the page language you chose, in your browser storage. Fonts and images are served from the site itself; there are no third-party scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any server, GitHub sees your IP address when the page loads.

The game (app.nightlex.com)

  • On your device: learning progress, settings, your own dictionary and drafts. It all stays in your browser storage and is not sent anywhere by itself.
  • What you send yourself: only when you press a button — “Test report”, “Report a problem” (a screenshot with a comment), the survey and the native-speaker language check. This goes to our server api.nightlex.com (Cloudflare, EU): the text, screenshot, language, time and a random device number — not your name. We do not store your IP address or browser details. Purpose: to find and fix mistakes.
  • Reminders: if you turn them on, the server stores your browser’s subscription address, the chosen time, time zone, language, day streak and the date of your last lesson — so it doesn’t remind you after you’ve already studied. Turning reminders off deletes this data.
  • Microphone: only after you press the button; speech recognition is done by your browser (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari).

Your rights

In the game: Settings → Privacy lets you export all your data to a file or delete everything. To delete what you sent to our server, write to us via “Me” → “Report a problem”. If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can contact Datatilsynet, the Danish data protection authority.
